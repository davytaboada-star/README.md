MY JOURNEY

My journey as an independent student in Cebu has taught me many important lessons. Living away from home gave me the opportunity to experience independence and learn how to handle my own responsibilities.

During this journey, I experienced financial challenges, school responsibilities, stress, and homesickness. However, these experiences also helped me become more responsible and prepared for the future.
