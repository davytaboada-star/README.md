WEEK 1 - STARTING MY INDEPENDENT LIFE

Week 1 was the beginning of my independent life in Cebu. Living independently was a new experience for me because I had to take care of many things on my own.

My Week 1 Experience

  • Living independently in Cebu
  • Adjusting to a new environment
  • Learning how to manage my own money
  • Becoming more responsible

My P1,000 Weekly Budget

I had a budget of P1,000 for one week. I needed to make this amount last as long as possible.

My money was mainly used for:

  • Food
  • School needs
  • Daily necessities

WEEK 2 - STRUGGLING WITH MY BUDGET

Week 2 became more difficult because I started experiencing more school-related expenses. I needed to buy things required for school and also deal with unexpected expenses.

Problems I Experienced

  • School-related expenses
  • Buying things needed for school
  • Unexpected expenses
  • P1,000 was not enough
  • Running short on money
  • Feeling stressed

What Week 2 Taught Me

I learned that I need to plan my budget better. I also learned how important it is to prioritize important needs and prepare for unexpected expenses.

WEEK 3 - MY SISTER VISITS

Week 3 was different because my sister came to Cebu. Her visit gave me a chance to take a break from studying and spend quality time with someone from my family.

Our Time Together

  • My sister came to Cebu
  • Took a break from studying
  • Spent quality time together
  • Hung out at a library
  • Talked and relaxed
  • Took my mind off school stress
  • Felt less lonely
  • Recharged and rested
  • Became motivated to continue studying
